Poway Woman's Club

A Digital Hub for Scholarships, Events, and Volunteers

Poway Woman's Club has served the community since 1960 — funding scholarships, supporting the Poway Library, and partnering with local historical and arts organizations. Our project modernizes how members and the public interact with the club online.

Current Club Website

Selection

We propose a Digital Hub for Poway Woman's Club, replacing scattered static pages with a functional, easy-to-update site. It combines:

  • Idea 1: Online Scholarship Portal - let students apply for the club's continuing education scholarship digitally instead of through paper forms.
  • Idea 2: Event & Volunteer Calendar - a shared calendar for general meetings, library support shifts, and community partner events.
One-sentence pitch: Give a 65-year-old community service club a modern, easy-to-maintain online presence that makes joining, volunteering, and applying for scholarships simple.

Why We Are Interested

We wanted a project with a real, local nonprofit that already does meaningful community work but is underserved by its current web presence. Poway Woman's Club has decades of real impact but an outdated site, making it a genuine opportunity to help rather than a hypothetical exercise.

User and Problem

Primary user: Prospective members, scholarship applicants, and current club members.

Problem: The club's current site is difficult to navigate and has no way to apply for scholarships or sign up for volunteer opportunities online.

Need: A simple, mobile-friendly hub for applications, events, and contact.
